# Pie Duel — quality audit (frozen build, 31 Oct 2026)

> Screenshots referenced below live in `docs/captures/audit/` on the auditor's machine; they are not tracked in git (17 MB).

Build: `http://localhost:4216/` (the `dist/` build; it has no music and no booth-1 coach yet, though both exist in `src/`).
Method: Playwright + Chrome (`--use-angle=metal`) at 1600×900 and 390×844 (touch), with real inputs only: keys `1/2/3/P`, clicks on `#goal-go`, `#explain-ok`, `.shop-card`, `#shop-go`, `#hand .card`, `#t4/#tgo`, `[data-bet]`, `#scale-track`, the result buttons and the midway tokens.
Game time was stepped with `advanceTime`. I ran the whole campaign once, L1 → L17, choosing **"לשלב הבא ›"** after each pass. I picked the solver's best move every turn, placed the call-it token, used Peek/Magnifier/Flavor at close odds and bought shop cards. At the midway after L8 I replayed L2, then went on to L9.
After the campaign I played one endless night. Separate probe runs tested deliberate wrong moves on L1, 3, 5, 6, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15 and 16, captured shot frame sequences, checked props left over from earlier levels, and played at phone width.
No console errors or page errors in any run. All 17 levels passed with 3★. L8 needed a rematch: the first attempt was lost and not passed, with 5/5 correct calls.

## 3. Art-direction critique: 5 most impactful upgrades

Reference bar: `docs/visual-targets/01–05.png`. The game currently reads as a clean prototype (flat purple walls, small busts, emoji UI); the mockups read as a lit, dense, painterly carnival.

1. **Rival presentation (largest gap).** Seat each rival behind the table at 1.3–1.5× the current scale, with the camera framing them chest-up at about ⅓ of the frame, to the right of the cannon, never behind it (compare `L05-b-decision.jpg` with mockup 01). Add a per-booth rival intro: a nameplate swing-in, one taunt line, and the "how they play" tell ("תמיד יורה בך", "משחקת לפי הסיכוי", "מאמין ברצפים"). Keep a **cream decal and a drip on the face and hat** until the next hit, so the score is readable on the rival. Big Mo needs his own silhouette (a sequined jacket is possible as a material swap plus a top hat and cape on the Bubu rig if there's no Tripo budget). Give the Twins distinct colours and props (bow tie vs cap). Use a lose pose under the result card, not a blurred background.
2. **Lighting, depth and materials.** Replace the flat purple walls with a layered backdrop: a painted far plane (Ferris wheel, tents, crowd silhouettes), a mid-ground of bokeh string lights, and a depth-of-field blur behind the rival. Light the rival with a warm amber key and a pink/cyan rim, and raise the bloom threshold so the cannon dome stops blowing out (`L01-b-decision.jpg`). Make the cannon brass properly metallic and reflective, and give it a real **glass hopper with the shells rolling inside** (mockup 01), not a floating jar. Add a varnish/specular pass on the table.
3. **Per-booth theming.** Today booths 1–4 are the same room and booth 5 is a void. Booth 1: a pastry stall (pie shelves, flour, red/cream). Booth 2: Grandma's card parlour (green felt, brass lamp, cat, purple drapes; a really dark night variant for L9). Booth 3: the Twins' chalkboard clubhouse (teal, drawn trees and tables on the boards: the subject *on the set*). Booth 4: a Robo arcade (orange, gears, a big wall-mounted wheel framed properly). Final: a true rooftop with a city skyline, spotlights, a crowd and fireworks. Change the music key and tempo per booth (the source already has per-booth waltzes; ship them).
4. **VFX and juice.** Cream as a fluid sprite or flipbook splash plus screen-lens splats (mockup 03 frames the cream card); confetti as small spinning paper with motion blur, not screen-filling quads (`J01-dare-1600.jpg`). Add an 80–120 ms hit-stop, a little screen shake, a slow-motion tracking camera on high-stakes shots, a close-up of the lever pull, shells dropping into the hopper on reload, and crowd cheers/boos. Stagger the "ספלאט!" comic and the reload banner so they never overlap.
5. **UI styling to match the mockups.** Carnival plaques with gold trim and glossy bevels (mockups 01/05). Shell icons inside a jar-shaped magazine panel. A **shop on the table under a spotlight** with painted cards and chip stacks (mockup 05), not flat rectangles. Stars stamped onto a ticket for the result. The midway as the 3D path map of mockup 04, with a topic label per booth ("בלי החזרה", "עצים וטבלאות", "עם החזרה") and a "חדש!" tag on each level's new mechanic.

## 4. Progression and difficulty critique

- **Replay and advancement work.** "לשלב הבא ›" chains all 17 levels. Any open level can be replayed from the midway (tested: L2 replay with a fresh shuffle, stars kept). Unlocking is linear, one level at a time, and booth by booth.
- **Topics do change by booth** (classical → without replacement and information → trees, tables, area → independence and frequency → mixed final). The *screen* rarely tells the player what is new, though (issue 5). The *decision* the player makes is the same in all 17 levels: "is the printed number above ½?" Because the meter and panels print the new quantity (issue 1), the hard part of each topic is skipped. Suggested fix: on levels that teach a new quantity, hide the meter until the player places the call-it token (or show the formula with blanks the player fills by tapping), then reveal the value and pay chips for a close call. Keep the meter as a reward or hint, not the default.
- **Difficulty barely rises.** Monte Carlo odds-play win rates are 44–83 % across all 17 levels with no trend, and pass rates are about 100 % everywhere. Rival skill rises (naive → odds → odds×2 → gambler → champion), but the mixes don't. Later booths should use mixes close to ½ (4/9, 5/9, 7/12), more shells, and fewer odds-meter levels (booths 3–4 have the meter on in 5 of 7 levels). L8 and L9 add almost nothing over L5–L7: booth 2 has 5 levels where 4 would do, while booth 4 (the richest content) has 3.
- **The transfer and test levels (4, 9, 13, 17)** are the only levels where the topic is practised unaided. They are worth signalling more strongly: a different frame, music and title card, and a "test passed" stamp.
- **No end-game hook.** After L17 there is no finale, no "you unlocked carnival nights" pointer and no star challenges (e.g. "3★ = all calls placed on the scale"). Endless nights unlock at L4 (too early: they spoil the Twins) and give no streak feedback beyond "שיא לילות ברצף: 0" on the title.
- **The pass rule can punish good play** (issue 6). Count a Peek-enabled correct call as informative when the call *before* the peek was informative, or credit "used the right card at ½" as a correct informative decision.
